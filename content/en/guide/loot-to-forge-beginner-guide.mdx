## Core Mechanics: The Basic Gameplay Loop Explained

At its heart, this Roblox experience relies on a simple yet addictive progression loop: gather materials, craft equipment, boost stats, and conquer deeper dungeon sectors. Every system connects directly to your character's baseline Power.

```
[Train Power] ➔ [Enter Dungeon Hallway] ➔ [Defeat Mobs & Gather Loot] ➔ [Fill Bag] ➔ [Forge Weapons & Armor / Sell for Coins] ➔ [Upgrade & Rebirth]
```

To jump into the action quickly, familiarize yourself with the default keybinds.

### Default Controls Overview

| Keybind / Input | Action / Purpose | Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **W, A, S, D** | Character Movement | Global |
| **Spacebar** | Jump / Traverse Obstacles | Global |
| **E** | Interact with NPCs, Forge, Stations | Hub Areas |
| **Q** | Activate Unique Weapon Skill | Combat / Dungeon |
| **Left Mouse Button (LMB)** | Manual Slash / Power Strike | Hub Training Area |

In the main dungeon hallway, your avatar automatically attacks adjacent foes. You do not need to click repeatedly to swing your blade. Instead, your primary focus should be positioning, managing crowd aggro, and timing your special skill with the **Q** key.

## Power Training: Manual vs. Auto-Training

Before venturing deep into the corridor, you must establish an initial Power pool. Power acts as your offensive baseline, directly influencing how quickly you can shred through dungeon mobs.

```
Total Combat Output = Baseline Power (Training) + Weapon Damage + Armor Modifiers + Rebirth Multipliers
```

You have two distinct methods to increase Power in the hub:

1. **Manual Training (LMB):** Actively swinging your blade provides immediate Power gains per click. While efficient for sudden boosts right after a rebirth, it requires active focus.
2. **Auto-Training Zone:** Stepping onto the dedicated auto-training platform allows your character to passively accumulate Power over time. This mechanic is ideal for semi-AFK sessions while you review your inventory or take a short break.

### Manual vs. Auto-Training Comparison

| Feature | Manual Training (LMB) | Auto-Training Zone |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Effort Required** | High (Active clicking) | None (Fully passive) |
| **Early Game Efficiency** | Fast burst scaling | Steady, consistent climb |
| **AFK Compatibility** | Requires macros or tools | Native in-game support |
| **Best Used For** | Post-rebirth recovery | Extended breaks & idle progression |

While training is vital early on, experienced players note that equipment upgrades yield dramatically higher multipliers later in the run. Always use training as a stepping stone to equip stronger swords.

## Dungeon Crawling, Bag Capacity, and Retreat Strategies

The dungeon is designed as a linear gauntlet segmented into increasingly difficult levels. Each room introduces tougher enemies with larger health pools and higher damage thresholds.

### Managing Backpack Capacity

Every defeated mob drops ores, crafting components, or sellable trinkets. However, your carry capacity is strictly limited at the start. Once your inventory reaches maximum capacity, you cannot pick up high-tier ores dropped by deeper enemies.

| Priority | Station / Action | Resource Needed | Primary Benefit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | Bag Capacity Upgrade | Coins | Carry more loot per run |
| **2** | Weapon Forge | Ores / Dungeon Drops | Exponential damage increase |
| **3** | Armor & Helmet Forge | Ores / Dungeon Drops | Base Power & survivability buffs |
| **4** | Enchantments | Coins | Bonus utility & passive damage procs |

Always visit the **Upgrades Station** in the central hub to expand your inventory before attempting deep runs. You can sell surplus, low-tier items to generate the Coins needed for these upgrades.

### The "Four-Hit Rule" for Retreating

One of the biggest mistakes highlighted in any **loot to forge beginner guide** is pushing too deep into high-level chambers without adequate gear. Dying inside the dungeon risks forfeiting unbanked resources collected during that specific run.

> **Player Rule of Thumb:** If a regular mob takes more than **four hits** to defeat, immediately disengage and return to the hub.

A high hit-to-kill ratio indicates that the next room's power scaling will likely overwhelm you. Head back to the hub, refine your weapons, and raise your baseline stats before pushing further.

## Weapon Forging, Armor Rolling, and Weapon Types

The Forge sits in the center of the hub and serves as your primary progression engine. By feeding ores gathered from your dungeon runs into the anvil, you can produce vastly superior gear.

### Katana vs. Greatsword

When forging weapons, you will encounter two primary styles, each offering unique offensive traits and distinct abilities activated via the **Q** key:

| Weapon Type | Attack Speed | Raw Damage per Hit | Playstyle / Role | Skill Archetype |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Katana** | Rapid | Moderate | Fast single-target shredding | Quick dashes, multi-hit slashes |
| **Greatsword** | Slower | High / Crushing | Clearing packs and tough elites | Wide AoE sweeps, heavy knockbacks |

Community reports suggest testing both archetypes to see which aligns with your rhythm. Katana builds excel at triggering on-hit enchantments rapidly, while Greatswords allow you to eliminate grouped hallway mobs in single swings.

### Armor and Helmets: Navigating RNG

Do not sleep on defense. By clicking over to the **Armor Tab** at the Forge, you can spend materials to craft protective gear. 
- Helmets and chestpieces directly augment your overall Power stats.
- The outcome between receiving a Helmet or an Armor piece is determined by RNG.
- Keep crafting until you fill both slots with your current tier's highest-grade ores.

### Enhancing Gear at the Enchants Station

Once you have secured a dependable Katana or Greatsword, take it to the Enchants Station. Spending your excess Coins here applies randomized enchantments that dramatically increase your damage output, grant gold find bonuses, or boost critical strike frequency.

## The Rebirth System: When and Why to Reset

Eventually, flat stat training and early-tier swords will hit diminishing returns. This is where the **Rebirth** system becomes your most crucial progression tool.

### What Rebirths Do

Rebirthing resets your current raw Power level back to starting values, but in exchange, it grants permanent, account-wide account multipliers:
- **Exponential Power Gain:** Future manual and auto-training sessions grant significantly more Power per tick.
- **Increased Coin Yields:** Enemies and sold loot reward far higher Coin payouts, accelerating your bag and enchantment upgrades.
- **Access to Higher Milestones:** Certain high-tier zones and mechanics require a minimum Rebirth count to enter.

### Rebirth Checklist

Before you hit the Rebirth button, make sure you have:
1. Sold off remaining low-tier ores to secure immediate Coins.
2. Kept your best forged weapons and armor equipped (equipment typically persists).
3. Prepared to immediately hop onto the Auto-Training platform to recover your baseline stats in minutes.

## Essential Tips for New Players

To wrap up this **loot to forge beginner guide**, keep these practical gameplay habits in mind during every session:

- **Rely on Auto-Attack, Focus on Spacing:** Let the game handle basic swings while you maneuver around mob packs to avoid taking unnecessary burst damage.
- **Prioritize Bag Upgrades Early:** A tiny backpack forces constant trips back to the hub. Expanding capacity means longer runs and far more high-tier loot per trip.
- **Sell Surplus Ores Immediately:** If an ore tier no longer produces weapon upgrades for your current level, sell it for Coins to fund your enchantments.
- **Use Skills on Cooldown:** Do not hoard your **Q** skill. Use it whenever it refreshes to quickly burst down elite mobs before they whittle away your health.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most important upgrade in this loot to forge beginner guide?
While weapons provide direct damage, upgrading your **Bag Capacity** is the most crucial early-game investment. A larger bag allows you to stay in deeper dungeon rooms longer, collect rare ores, and earn substantially more Coins per run.

### How do I trigger special skills during combat?
Equip a Katana or Greatsword and press the **Q** key on your keyboard. The skill performed depends on the weapon archetype and the materials used to forge it.

### Does Rebirth delete my forged swords and armor?
Rebirth resets your raw Power level to baseline, but your forged equipment, bag capacity upgrades, and unlocked perks remain intact. Always equip your best gear immediately after rebirthing to accelerate your recovery.

### Why am I dying so quickly in the linear hallway dungeon?
You have likely pushed beyond your current gear tier. If regular hallway enemies take more than four hits to eliminate, your damage output is too low. Return to the hub, forge updated weapons and armor, and spend time training your Power before re-entering.
