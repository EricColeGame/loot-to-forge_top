## Core Gameplay Loop and Basic Controls

The heartbeat of +1 Loot to Forge is an addictive loop: train your character's Power, step into a linear dungeon hallway, defeat waves of monsters to hoard valuable ores, and bring those materials back to the central hub to forge upgraded equipment. 

Unlike traditional action RPGs where manual dodging and manual swinging take center stage, combat inside the dungeon operates on an auto-attack mechanic. Your avatar swings automatically when in melee range of enemies. Your primary active responsibilities involve precise positioning, tracking incoming monster damage, activating weapon skills, and managing bag capacity.

### Essential Control Scheme

Before descending into the hallway, make sure your keybinds are second nature:

| Key / Input | In-Hub Function | Dungeon Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **W, A, S, D** | Character Movement | Movement and Positioning |
| **Spacebar** | Jump / Traverse Obstacles | Jump / Reposition around mobs |
| **E** | Interact with NPCs, Forge, and Stations | Pickup prompts / Exit gates |
| **Q** | Weapon Preview | Activate Equipped Weapon Skill |
| **Left Mouse Button (LMB)** | Manual Power Training Slash | Camera Control / Target Lock |

When exploring the hub, manual slashes give small increments to your baseline Power rating. However, actively clicking isn't your only path to baseline progression.

## Power Training: Active vs. AFK Progression

Power acts as your core stat multiplier, increasing the raw damage output applied through your forged weapons. There are two main ways to increase your base Power before entering the dungeon: manual training and the auto-training zone.

Community reports and player testing show that relying solely on manual swinging quickly leads to diminishing returns once you advance past the earliest levels. Utilizing the auto-training area allows your avatar to passively accumulate Power while you manage inventory, review enchantments, or step away from the keyboard entirely.

| Training Method | Effort Required | Power Gain Rate | Best Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Manual Clicking (LMB)** | High (Active Input) | Moderate (Early game) | Level 1–10 quick-start |
| **AFK Auto-Training Zone** | None (Passive) | Consistent / Scaleable | Overnight or crafting downtime |
| **Dungeon Clearing** | High (Semi-Active) | Exponential (via Loot) | Primary gameplay loop |

While passive training provides a solid safety net, the real power spikes come from dungeon ores and equipment crafting. For players tracking official platform updates or bug fixes, keep an eye on the official [Roblox platform status](https://status.roblox.com/) during major patch rollouts.

## Dungeon Exploration and the "Four-Hit Rule"

The dungeon in +1 Loot to Forge is structured as a linear series of escalating levels. As you cross floor thresholds, enemy health pools and offensive power scale dramatically. 

### Knowing When to Retreat

One of the most critical habits emphasized in this **loot to forge guide** is tactical retreat. Dying deep inside the dungeon risks losing your hard-earned unbanked materials. Community testing has established a reliable benchmark known as the **Four-Hit Rule**:

* If regular enemies on your current floor require **more than four auto-attacks** to defeat, your damage output is falling behind the curve.
* Continuing forward into the next segment will likely result in your avatar being swarmed and defeated before your bag is full.
* As soon as you hit this threshold, immediately retreat to the hub, process your collected ores at the forge, and purchase carry capacity upgrades.

### Dungeon Hazard and Monster Scaling

| Floor Tier | Common Drops | Enemy Scaling | Recommended Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Floors 1–5** | Copper, Basic Scrap, Low Ores | Low HP, Single Targets | Clear quickly; fill starter bags |
| **Floors 6–15** | Iron, Silver, Rare Cores | Moderate HP, Clustered Spawns | Use Q skills for crowd control |
| **Floors 16–30** | Gold, Mithril, Enchant Shards | High Damage, High Armor | Retreat if hitting > 4 times per mob |
| **Floors 31+** | Exotic Alloys, Radiant Ores | Boss-type HP bars | Require enchanted Greatswords and full armor sets |

## The Forge: Crafting Swords, Katanas, and Armor

Once your bag is full of ores, return to the central Forge. Crafting breaks down into two primary equipment categories: **Weapons** and **Armor**.

### Weapon Types: Katanas vs. Greatswords

When forging blades, the materials you use determine both base attack stats and the skill bound to your **Q** key. There are two primary weapon archetypes in the game:

1. **Katanas:** Feature higher attack speeds and mobility-oriented active skills. Ideal for clearing large packs of low-to-medium health enemies quickly.
2. **Greatswords:** Trade attack speed for devastating per-hit damage and wide-cleave active skills. Greatswords are the undisputed kings of boss-clearing and breaking through high-armor thresholds.

| Weapon Category | Attack Cadence | Skill Type (Q Key) | Ideal Playstyle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Katana** | Rapid (High swings/sec) | Dash slashes / Multi-hit flurry | Farming floors where mobs die in 1–2 hits |
| **Greatsword** | Slow (Heavy impact) | Ground slam / High-damage cleave | Pushing new, high-difficulty dungeon floors |

### Crafting Helmets and Armor Pieces

Many beginner players focus solely on weapons and completely ignore defensive gear. To craft protective items, toggle over to the **Armor tab** inside the Forge interface. 

Armor pieces—divided into Helmets and Chest Armor—directly boost your overall Power multiplier and survivability. Note that receiving either a Helmet or a Chestpiece from the forge relies on internal RNG. Keep crafting until you have high-tier pieces occupying both slots.

## Upgrades and Weapon Enchantments

Raw ores aren't just for forging; surplus materials should be liquidated into Coins. Coins are the vital currency needed to scale your overall efficiency through the Upgrades Station and the Enchants Station.

### Essential Upgrade Priorities

If you want to maximize your progression pace, spend your Coins in this exact order:

1. **Bag Capacity (Carry Capacity):** Your dungeon runs are strictly limited by your inventory space. A larger bag means fewer trips back to the hub and more ores collected per run.
2. **Coin Multipliers:** Boosts the amount of gold received from selling excess scrap and common ores.
3. **Speed Upgrades:** Reduces travel downtime inside the linear hallway.

### Enchanting Your Weapons

Once you forge an optimal Katana or Greatsword, take it directly to the Enchantment station. Enchants apply passive modifiers that fundamentally alter your combat performance:

| Enchantment Type | Primary Benefit | Recommended Weapon Pairing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sharpness / Raw Damage** | Flat percentage boost to all weapon swings | Greatswords (amplifies massive hits) |
| **Haste / Swiftness** | Increases overall swing speed | Greatswords (mitigates slow animations) |
| **Greed / Fortune** | Bonus coin and ore drop rates on kill | Katanas (rapid low-floor farming) |
| **Critical Strike** | Chance to deal 2x–3x damage on auto-attack | Both weapon archetypes |

## Rebirth Mechanics: Unlocking Massive Power Multipliers

Eventually, equipment upgrades and training gains will begin to plateau. This is where the Rebirth system becomes your most vital tool. A comprehensive **loot to forge guide** must emphasize that hoarding Power without rebirthing severely slows down long-term progression.

When you undergo a Rebirth, your current Power level resets back to base. In exchange, you unlock permanent global multipliers applied directly to your Power gain rate and Coin income.

| Rebirth Tier | Base Power Requirement | Permanent Multipliers Awarded | Unlocked Perks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | 10,000 Power | 2x Power Gain, 1.5x Coins | Access to Tier 2 Forge recipes |
| **Tier 2** | 100,000 Power | 4x Power Gain, 2.5x Coins | Unlocks advanced Enchantment pools |
| **Tier 3** | 1,000,000 Power | 8x Power Gain, 5.0x Coins | Increased base bag storage limits |
| **Tier 4+** | 10,000,000+ Power | Exponential scaling | Endgame dungeon floor entry permissions |

As a rule of thumb, trigger your Rebirth the very moment you reach the required Power threshold unless you are only a few ores away from forging an entirely new tier of armor or weaponry. The subsequent run back to your previous floor will be significantly faster thanks to the newly acquired multipliers.

## Step-by-Step Progression Checklist

Follow this quick checklist during your play sessions to ensure you are progressing efficiently:

1. **Fill Your Bag:** Enter the dungeon hallway and auto-attack until inventory capacity hits 100%.
2. **Respect the Four-Hit Rule:** If mobs take 5+ swings to eliminate, retreat immediately.
3. **Smelt and Forge:** Visit the Forge. Craft your highest available weapon tier, then switch to the Armor tab to roll for missing gear pieces.
4. **Liquidate Leftovers:** Sell leftover, lower-tier ores for Coins.
5. **Upgrade Bag Space:** Invest surplus Coins into Bag Capacity at the Upgrade station.
6. **Enchant Active Weapons:** Roll for high-impact damage or speed enchants.
7. **Rebirth Immediately:** As soon as the Rebirth button lights up, claim your permanent bonuses and repeat the loop.

Using this structured approach ensures you never waste time grinding under-leveled content or entering floors you aren't prepared to clear.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most efficient way to use this loot to forge guide for beginners?
The fastest route for beginners is to prioritize Bag Capacity upgrades above all else. A larger bag lets you hoard more ores per dungeon run, which rapidly speeds up both weapon crafting and Coin generation during the early levels.

### When should I choose a Greatsword over a Katana?
Choose a Katana when you are speed-farming lower floors where enemies die in one or two hits. Switch to a Greatsword when pushing deeper floors or challenging tough mob packs, as its high base damage and wide cleave skill make it much better at bypassing heavy armor.

### Do I lose my crafted weapons and armor when I Rebirth?
No. Undergoing a Rebirth resets your raw Power stat, but you retain your forged weapons, armor pieces, permanent upgrades, and earned currencies. Your retained high-tier gear allows you to reclaim your lost Power levels in a fraction of the time.

### How does weapon skill damage scale in +1 Loot to Forge?
Weapon skills (activated with the **Q** key) scale directly based on your current Power level and the quality of the materials used to forge the blade. Using higher-tier ores yields skills with broader areas of effect and significantly higher damage scaling.
