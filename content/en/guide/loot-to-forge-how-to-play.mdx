## Core Gameplay Loop and Mechanics

At its core, +1 Loot to Forge is an incremental dungeon crawler designed around a high-tempo progression loop. Players spawn into a central hub, build baseline strength, delve into a linear hallway filled with scaling enemies, and extract with ores to craft deadlier armaments.

```
[ Hub: Train & Rebirth ] ──> [ Linear Dungeon: Defeat Mobs ]
          ▲                                    │
          │                                    ▼
[ Upgrades & Enchanting ] <── [ Forge Gear / Sell Loot ]
```

The progression cycle can be broken down into four distinct phases:
1. **Preparation:** Generating baseline Power via active clicking or utilizing the automated training zone in the hub.
2. **Dungeon Delving:** Pushing through segmented rooms where mobs yield valuable ores and crafting items.
3. **Inventory Management:** Returning to town before your bag capacity overflows, converting excess spoils into Coins.
4. **Forging and Enhancement:** Crafting new weapons, rolling for defensive armor, and applying enchantments to multiply your damage output.

To keep track of basic controls while navigating between the sanctuary and the combat zones, consult the control layout below:

| Action | Keybinding (PC) | Context / Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Movement** | W, A, S, D | Standard directional navigation |
| **Jump** | Spacebar | Clear small obstacles and terrain |
| **Interact** | E | Speak to NPCs, open Forge, access stations |
| **Skill Activation** | Q | Cast your forged weapon's special ability |
| **Basic Attack (Hub)** | Left Mouse Button (LMB) | Swing weapon manually to increase Power |

Inside the dungeon, combat is streamlined: your character executes basic swings automatically against nearby threats, allowing you to focus your attention on monster spacing, threat evaluation, and skill timing.

## Power Generation: Active Swings vs. Auto-Training

Before venturing deep into the crypts, you need sufficient Power to cut down dungeon sentries quickly. Power acts as your baseline damage multiplier, compounding the attack values granted by your equipment.

The game offers two primary methods for raising this stat:

| Training Method | Input Required | Power Gain Rate | Best Used For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Active Slashing** | Constant LMB clicks in Hub | High (Immediate) | Quick stat spikes before entering early floors |
| **Auto-Training Zone** | Passive standing in designated ring | Moderate (Consistent) | AFK farming, breaks, or multitasking |

While training is critical early on, player experience indicates that raw weapon stats soon outstrip base training gains. Think of training as the foundational base that makes top-tier weapons hit with world-shattering force.

## Mastering Combat, Weapons, and Skills

Once you step beyond the hub gates, you enter a linear dungeon sequence where every succeeding floor features enemies with exponentially higher health pools and damage thresholds. 

Understanding weapon mechanics is vital when learning **loot to forge how to play** at an advanced level. The game features two distinct primary weapon classes:

| Weapon Type | Attack Cadence | Skill Profile (Q Key) | Playstyle Suitability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Katana** | Rapid multi-hits | High-frequency, single-target or narrow sweep | Best for burning down high-health lone enemies |
| **Greatsword** | Heavy, deliberate cleaves | Wide area-of-effect (AoE) crowd control | Ideal for clearing dense clusters of dungeon mobs |

Your special attack (bound to **Q**) is determined by two factors: the weapon classification and the quality of ores utilized during the blacksmithing process. Combining rare ores creates skills with amplified area radius, status ailments, or raw burst damage.

### The Four-Hit Retreat Rule

One of the most frequent mistakes made by beginners is pushing blindly into deeper dungeon rooms. Unlike standard RPGs, the stat gap between consecutive dungeon levels ramps up drastically. 

Community reports suggest adopting the **Four-Hit Rule**:
- If normal hallway enemies require more than **four swings** to eliminate, you have reached your current gear limit.
- Pushing further puts you at imminent risk of defeat, which can dump your unbanked backpack resources.
- The moment enemies absorb four hits without falling, immediately fall back to the hub to bank materials, sell surplus ores, and craft an upgraded blade.

## Forging, Armor Crafting, and Bag Upgrades

The town forge is where gathered resources turn into real combat prowess. By bringing ores extracted from dungeon runs to the central anvil, you can forge new weapons and roll for protective equipment.

| Crafting Category | Primary Cost | Stat Benefits | Key Mechanics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weapons** | Dungeon Ores | Direct Attack Damage & Skill Unlocks | Choice of Katana or Greatsword |
| **Armor / Helmets** | Dungeon Ores (Armor Tab) | Significant Power Multipliers | Random outcome (RNG decides piece) |
| **Bag Capacity** | Gold Coins (Upgrade Shop) | Increases maximum inventory slots | Essential for longer dungeon dives |
| **Enchantments** | Gold Coins (Enchants Shop) | Adds percentage stat perks to gear | Rerollable traits to optimize builds |

Armor crafting is just as crucial as weapon production. Although the anvil determines whether you receive a helmet or chest armor based on RNG, the raw Power bonuses provided by armor pieces are necessary to break through mid-game stat walls.

To fund continuous bag and enchantment upgrades, sell low-tier ores directly at the town vendor. Increasing your backpack capacity should always take financial priority over early enchantments, as carrying more loot per run directly accelerates your crafting cadence. ## Rebirth System and Long-Term Progression

Once your standard upgrades begin showing diminishing returns, the Rebirth system becomes your primary path forward. Rebirth resets your current Power level back to baseline in exchange for permanent account-wide multipliers.

| Rebirth Tier Stage | Requirement Threshold | Primary Rewards | Recommended Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Early Rebirth (1–3)** | Low-to-mid Power targets | +Power gain rate, minor Coin boost | Trigger as soon as target is reached |
| **Mid Rebirth (4–8)** | Substantial Power scaling | Massive Coin multipliers, trait slots | Hoard crafting ores before resetting |
| **Endgame Rebirths** | Exponential stat targets | Endgame area access, mastery scaling | Optimize enchanted gear sets first |

When you initiate a rebirth, your raw Power is wiped clean, but you keep high-tier weapons, armor, and unlocked storage upgrades. Because your newly forged blades retain their high damage figures, climbing back through early dungeon floors takes a fraction of the time, allowing you to harvest late-game ores much faster than on your initial run.

## Essential Step-by-Step Tips for New Delvers

To streamline your playthrough, follow this operational checklist during your first few hours:

1. **Leverage the Auto-Trainer Early:** Before taking your first step into the dungeon, stand in the auto-training circle until you build enough Power to one-shot early corridor mobs.
2. **Prioritize Bag Space Over Weapon Rolls:** Your ability to stay inside the dungeon determines your income. Keep bag space upgraded so you aren't forced to backtrack every sixty seconds.
3. **Double-Check Skill Tooltips:** Different ore recipes grant varying utility to your **Q** ability. Experiment with component blends to find an ability that covers wide angles.
4. **Enchant Only High-Rarity Weapons:** Do not spend precious Coins enchanting basic starter blades. Save your enchantment funds for weapons forged from deep-level ores.
5. **Rebirth Without Hesitation:** Many new players fear losing their Power stat. However, the multiplicative bonuses gained from rebirths vastly outweigh temporary stat losses.

Following this structured loop demystifies **loot to forge how to play**, letting you quickly transform from a novice with a rusty blade into an unstoppable dungeon sweeper.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the fastest way to get coins in the game?
The quickest way to accumulate Coins is to maximize your backpack capacity at the Upgrade station, run through the deepest dungeon floor you can clear comfortably, and sell all surplus low-tier ores at the hub merchant.

### Can I choose whether I forge a helmet or body armor?
No. When spending materials in the Armor tab at the Forge, the piece you craft (Helmet or Armor) is determined randomly by RNG. Both items provide substantial Power boosts, making either outcome a net upgrade to your total stats.

### What should I do if dungeon enemies defeat me too quickly?
If you are taking excessive damage or failing to defeat mobs within four hits, immediately return to the hub. Spend time in the auto-training area to elevate your base Power, roll for upgraded armor pieces, or enchant your primary weapon with bonus damage perks before re-entering.

### In Loot to Forge how to play, what weapon type is better: Katana or Greatsword?
Neither weapon is strictly superior; they serve different combat roles. Katanas excel at rapid-fire single-target damage, making them lethal against tough dungeon blockers. Greatswords feature wider cleaving arcs and crowd-control abilities, making them far superior for sweeping through packed corridors of weaker enemies.
