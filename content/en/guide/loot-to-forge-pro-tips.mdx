## Combat Strategy and the Four-Hit Retreat Rule

One of the most important **loot to forge pro tips** shared by veteran players involves damage pacing. In this incremental dungeon crawler, sudden enemy stat spikes between linear levels can catch you off guard, resulting in instant deaths that forfeit your unbanked inventory.

### The "Four-Hit" Indicator

To prevent tragic wipeouts deep in the dungeon, community reports recommend the **Four-Hit Threshold**:

1. **One to Two Hits (Safe Clear):** You are over-geared or properly scaled for the room. Push forward aggressively.
2. **Three to Four Hits (Efficiency Warning):** Your kill rate is slowing. Enemies begin dealing chip damage, and your clear speed drops noticeably.
3. **Five or More Hits (Immediate Retreat):** Do not step into the next sector. Turn back to the central hub, bank your gathered ores, sell excess clutter, and craft next-tier equipment.

| Weapon Family | Primary Skill (`Q`) | Playstyle Advantage | Optimal Scenario |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Katana** | Rapid Multi-Strike / Dash | High attack frequency, agile positioning | Level clearing, swarming smaller mobs |
| **Greatsword** | Heavy Cleave / Impact Wave | Massive burst damage, armor-cracking hits | Tough corridor mini-bosses, bulkier targets |

The skill linked to your `Q` key is determined by both the weapon frame and the quality of the materials fed into the forge. Save high-impact Greatsword bursts for high-health wall encounters to maintain momentum.

## Crafting Optimization: Weapons, Armor, and Enchantments

Dumping every raw material into random sword crafts is a beginner pitfall. To execute refined **loot to forge pro tips**, you must strategically divide your harvested ores between mainhand weapons and defensive gear.

### Balancing Weapons and Defensive Armor

The Forge features both a weapon fabrication tab and an Armor tab. While high-damage blades allow faster enemy clears, armor pieces (Helmets and Chestplates) directly bolster your foundational Power pool.

Because the system relies on RNG when determining whether you receive a Helmet or a piece of Armor, never spend your entire ore reserve on one piece. Stagger your crafts so you can equip both slots evenly before heading back down into deeper zones.

| Gear Category | How to Acquire | Main Benefit | Crafting Risk Factor |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weapons** | Hub Forge (Ores) | Damage output, attack scaling, `Q` Skill | Low (Direct stat improvement) |
| **Helmets** | Forge Armor Tab (RNG) | Large passive Power multiplier | Medium (RNG roll vs. Chest armor) |
| **Chest Armor** | Forge Armor Tab (RNG) | Base Power and survivability booster | Medium (RNG roll vs. Helmet) |
| **Enchantments** | Hub Enchant Station (Coins)| Specialized combat procs and stat bonuses | Low to Medium (Coin sink) |

### Enchantment Synergy

Once you forge a high-tier Greatsword or Katana, visit the Enchants station. Enchantments cost Coins—earned directly by selling surplus dungeon junk. Applying an enchantment to an outdated weapon is a common waste of gold; only enchant weapons that you plan to carry through at least two subsequent Rebirth stages.

| Priority Rank | Upgrade Station Focus | Investment Reason |

## Mastering the Rebirth Loop

Rebirthing resets your base Power to zero in exchange for massive, permanent multipliers that affect both Power regeneration and total Coin revenue. Hesitating on your Rebirth significantly slows your long-term progression.

Applying smart **loot to forge pro tips** means knowing the exact conditions for pulling the trigger on a Rebirth:

1. **Pre-Rebirth Stockpiling:** Ensure you have enough high-grade ore sitting in your reserve bag to immediately craft a decent weapon the moment your Power resets.
2. **Instant Re-gear:** After hitting the Rebirth button, craft an introductory blade using your banked materials. This lets you skip the tedious initial training slashes and head straight back into the early dungeon rooms.
3. **Multiplier Stacking:** The Coin boost from Rebirths accelerates your backpack upgrades exponentially, turning 10-minute early-game farming loops into sub-two-minute sprints.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the best loot to forge pro tips for brand-new players?
Focus entirely on your bag capacity and adhere strictly to the Four-Hit Retreat Rule. If an enemy takes more than four swings to defeat, return to the hub immediately to avoid losing your inventory.

### How do I unlock better skills on my weapons?
Your `Q` ability is decided by the weapon type (Katana vs. Greatsword) and the quality of the ores consumed during the crafting process at the hub Forge. Rarer dungeon drops unlock deadlier skill animations.

### Is it better to train manually or explore the dungeon?
Dungeon diving is always the superior method for active progression because it yields both ores and coins. The auto-training area is primarily intended for passive gains when you need to step away from the screen.

### When is the ideal time to perform a Rebirth?
Execute a Rebirth the moment you meet the Power threshold, provided you have a small reserve of spare crafting ores to forge a starter weapon right away. The permanent multipliers far outweigh any temporary loss of raw Power.
