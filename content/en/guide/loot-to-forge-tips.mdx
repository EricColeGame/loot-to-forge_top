## Core Mechanics and Essential Controls

Before heading deep into hostile territory, you must understand how movement, attacking, and skills function in the hub versus active dungeon halls. Unlike open-world simulators, combat pacing relies heavily on positioning and timely retreats rather than constant manual clicking.

### Input Mapping and Interface

Your character attacks automatically while navigating dungeon floors, freeing your hands to focus on skill timing and distance control. The central hub, however, allows manual swings to build raw Power.

| Key / Action | In-Hub Function | Dungeon Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **W, A, S, D** | Standard movement around vendors | Mobility and positioning around mobs |
| **Spacebar** | Jump onto training platforms | Dodging ground strikes and repositioning |
| **E** | Interact with Forge, Upgrades, Enchants | Pick up dropped loot and interact with portals |
| **Q** | Preview equipped skill animations | Cast weapon-specific active skill |
| **Left Click** | Manual blade swing (Power generation) | Auto-attack override (passive default) |

## The Four-Hit Rule and Dungeon Exploration

Survival hinges on knowing when to press forward and when to turn back. Dying deep inside a run clears unbanked resources, completely stalling your account's advancement.

### The Retreat Threshold

A common mistake is pushing "just one more floor" when basic enemies survive extended attack sequences. The stat jump between successive hallway tiers is exponential rather than linear.

| Enemy Health State | Hits to Defeat | Risk Assessment | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Trivial** | 1 Hit | Zero Risk | Sprint through; gather all ores |
| **Optimal** | 2–3 Hits | Low Risk | Farm steadily until bag reaches capacity |
| **Dangerous** | 4 Hits | High Risk | Finish current pack, collect drops, immediately retreat |
| **Fatal** | 5+ Hits | Extreme Risk | Do not engage; return to hub to upgrade/forge |

Always keep an eye on your bag capacity indicator. The moment your pack fills, extra enemy drops vanish into thin air. Pushing deeper with a full bag yields zero crafting progress and exposes you to fatal wipeouts.

## Mastering the Forge: Weapons and Armor RNG

The Forge serves as your primary driver for account progression. Training gives you baseline numbers, but the multipliers attached to forged Katana, Greatsword, and armor pieces define your actual combat performance.

### Katana vs. Greatsword Playstyles

Different weapon archetypes provide distinct skill animations and attack rhythms when triggered via the **Q** key. Matching your weapon choice to your playstyle maximizes room-clearing speed.

| Weapon Archetype | Attack Speed | Skill Focus (Q Key) | Ideal For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Katana** | Rapid | Wide-arc multi-slash, fast cooldowns | Clearing dense clusters of smaller enemies quickly |
| **Greatsword** | Heavy / Slow | High burst, single-target slam, stun | Slaying beefy floor mini-bosses and high-HP targets |

### Armor and Helmet Crafting

Do not spend all your gathered ores strictly on offensive weapons. Navigating to the Forge's **Armor** tab provides access to defensive gear that dramatically enhances your overall Power stat. 

Crafting in this category uses random roll mechanics:
- **Helmets:** Grant defensive buffers and overall Power percentage boosts.
- **Chest Armor:** Provides massive baseline Power increases that scale with training.
- **RNG Distribution:** Player experience suggests roughly a 50/50 roll rate between Helmets and Armor pieces; keep crafting until your lower-stat slot is replaced with upgraded materials.

## Gold Management: Inventory Upgrades and Enchantments

Every successful dungeon run fills your pack with surplus ores and lower-tier materials. Selling surplus loot generates Coins, the essential currency used across various town upgrade stations.

### Priority Order for Gold Spending

Misallocating your early Coins will significantly slow your pacing. Follow this strict investment order to keep your run-to-forge loop moving swiftly:

```
[1. Bag Capacity Upgrades] ---> [2. Weapon Enchantments] ---> [3. Excess Rerolls]
```

1. **Bag Capacity (Top Priority):** Maximizing your inventory volume allows you to stay in higher dungeon tiers longer, collecting top-tier ores without having to return every two minutes.
2. **Weapon Enchantments (Secondary):** Spending Coins at the Enchants station rolls passive modifiers directly onto your active sword, multiplying damage, crit chance, or coin drops.
3. **Stat Rerolls (Late Early-Game):** Only spend excess currency on rerolling weapon skills once your backpack can comfortably hold 30+ items.

## Power Farming: Manual Slashing vs. Auto-Training

Raising your baseline Power stat dictates how high your weapon scaling reaches. Relying solely on dungeon kills leaves you under-leveled when boss barriers appear.

| Training Method | Input Required | Power Gain Rate | Best Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Manual Clicking (Hub)** | Active clicking | High burst gain | Pre-dungeon prep or pushing the final 5% before a Rebirth |
| **Auto-Training Zone** | Completely passive | Steady, automated gain | Overnight AFK or taking breaks between extended dungeon crawls |
| **Dungeon Mob Slaying** | Directional control | Moderate + Loot drops | Active gameplay loop when upgrading gear |

A proven approach among top players is combining auto-training sessions with targeted dungeon dives: leave your avatar in the auto-training area when away from your keyboard, then use that accumulated Power to push several floors deeper once you return to forge new armaments.

## The Rebirth Strategy: When to Reset Your Power

Rebirth is the ultimate progression engine in the game. Resetting your current Power level might feel counterintuitive after spending hours grinding, but the permanent account multipliers are vital for reaching deep dungeon floors.

### Rebirth Threshold Decision Matrix

Executing a Rebirth resets your raw Power counter to zero while keeping your unlocked features and granting massive permanent buffs.

| Player Power Level | Floor Clear Speed | Gold Reserves | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Below Target Requirement** | Fast (Floors 1–5) | Low | Keep farming; do not worry about resets yet |
| **Target Requirement Met** | Sluggish (4+ hits per mob) | High | **Rebirth immediately.** The permanent multiplier will skyrocket your recovery speed |
| **Target Met, Strong Gear** | Fast (1–2 hits per mob) | Moderate | Push 2–3 floors higher to gather elite ores for immediate post-reset crafting, then Rebirth |

Following these structured **loot to forge tips** prevents players from resetting too early without materials or waiting too long while stuck in high-scaling slogs.

Keep this quick reference sheet handy whenever you feel your damage dropping off during dungeon runs:

- [ ] **Empty Bag Before Leaving:** Sell unneeded low-tier common ores to keep your inventory ready for high-rarity metals.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the most important loot to forge tips for complete beginners?
The most impactful **loot to forge tips** for new adventurers are strictly obeying the "four-hit retreat rule," prioritizing bag capacity upgrades with early gold, and never ignoring the Armor tab at the Forge. Keeping your bag upgraded ensures you collect enough ore to craft game-changing weapons every single run.

### Should I forge a Katana or a Greatsword first?
For general floor clearing, the Katana is typically preferred early on due to its rapid attack animations and wide-clearing **Q** skill. Switch to a Greatsword once you encounter dense, high-health barrier enemies where burst damage is required to avoid taking heavy incoming hits.

### Does performing a Rebirth delete my forged swords and armor?
No. While Rebirth resets your current baseline Power level, it applies permanent multipliers to your future Power generation and Coin earnings, making your subsequent climb back through the dungeon floors significantly faster.

### How do I earn Coins quickly to upgrade my carry capacity?
Coins are acquired by selling excess, low-rarity loot at the hub vendors. After running higher dungeon floors, sell obsolete common ores and gear while reserving only the rarest materials for your next round of crafting.
