### When to Trigger a Rebirth

Rebirth resets your accumulated Power pool in exchange for an enduring multiplier. Triggering a rebirth too early wastes setup time, while delaying it forces you to endure slow, diminishing click returns. 

The ideal moment to rebirth is when you can no longer comfortably unlock the next training zone and Stage 8 enemies defeat you rapidly. Remember: **Forged weapons remain in your backpack through a rebirth**. Deposit any remaining loose minerals into the cauldron to craft top equipment before activating the reset.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most efficient recipe for +1 Loot to Forge best items early on?
The most reliable early-to-mid game recipe combines 1x Gold, 1x Obsidian, 1x Sapphire, and 1x Fire Crystal. This combination crafts the Epic **Phantom Violet Katana**, providing the burst DPS needed to farm Stage 7 Jackal Zombies cleanly.

### Should I sell my rare gems for gold upgrades?
Never sell Gold, Obsidian, Sapphire, or Fire Crystals early on. Instead, sell common junk drops like surplus Sunfire Ore, low-grade Emeralds, and basic Mystic Crystals to fund your Max Ore Count upgrades and enchanting costs.

### Do my crafted weapons disappear when I use Rebirth?
No. Your weapons, armor, and equipment stored in your Backpack persist through a Rebirth. Only your accumulated clicking Power resets in exchange for a permanent multiplier. Always forge your loose ores into weapons before initiating a rebirth.

### Why is my Epic weapon dealing low damage to Stage 8 mobs?
If your weapon rarity is sufficient, low damage usually points to an undertrained base Power stat. Visit the Tier 3 (x15) or Tier 4 (x100) training zones to rapidly scale your base stats, or perform a Rebirth to secure a permanent global damage boost.
