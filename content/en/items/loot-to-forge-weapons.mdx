## The Core Loop: Slaying Mobs and Harvesting Materials

At its foundation, your progression relies on a tightly integrated gameplay loop. You venture into a linear, floor-segmented dungeon, slice through progressively stronger enemy mobs, collect fallen materials, and bring them back to the central hub to craft superior equipment.

```
Enter Dungeon ➔ Auto-Attack Mobs ➔ Fill Bag with Loot ➔ Return to Hub ➔ Forge Weapons / Armor ➔ Rebirth & Repeat
```

Combat within the dungeon is automated—your character swings automatically at nearby threats, leaving you free to focus on positioning, skill execution, and spatial awareness. The items that fall from defeated foes consist primarily of craftable ores and valuable salvage. However, you cannot farm endlessly in a single run; your carrying capacity is strictly governed by your bag size. Once your bag hits maximum weight, you must return to the hub to process your haul.

## Weapon Archetypes: Katanas vs. Greatswords

When you bring your collected loot to forge weapons at the central station, you are typically crafting one of two primary weapon families: **Katanas** or **Greatswords**. Both weapon categories feature dedicated skills triggered via the **Q key**, but they cater to very different combat rhythms and playstyles.

### Comparing Weapon Classes

| Attribute | Katana Archetype | Greatsword Archetype |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Attack Speed** | High | Slow |
| **Damage per Swing** | Moderate | Very High |
| **Active Skill (Q)** | Rapid Flurry / Dash Slash | Heavy Ground Slam / AOE Cleave |
| **Ideal Engagement** | Mobile skirmishing, single targets | Clustered packs, burst encounters |
| **Synergy Need** | Flat Power buffs, bleed/crit procs | Percentage Power buffs, stun perks |
| **Forging Focus** | High-velocity ores (Silver, Mithril) | Dense structural ores (Iron, Void Core) |

The materials you feed into the anvil alter the elemental properties and secondary skill modifiers of the finished blade. A Katana forged with high-tier rare ores can shred single targets in fractions of a second, while a dense Greatsword helps you clear clustered walls of enemies before they can retaliate.

## Combat Survival: The Four-Hit Rule and Safe Farming

While auto-attacking simplifies dungeon movement, survivability requires careful threat assessment. The Power gap between successive dungeon levels can cause sudden, lethal spikes in damage taken. If you push into a room where enemies overwhelm your defenses, you risk dying and forfeiting unbanked inventory.

To safeguard your progress, follow the **Four-Hit Rule**:

1. **Test the Mob:** Engage the first enemy in a newly reached level.
2. **Count the Blows:** If the monster does not fall within **four basic attacks**, your current Power rating is insufficient for that floor.
3. **Trigger Skill (Q):** Use your active skill to finish off the current group safely.
4. **Immediate Retreat:** Return to the hub immediately. Do not test deeper rooms with sub-optimal damage.

```
Engage Enemy ➔ More than 4 Hits to Kill? ➔ YES ➔ Evacuate to Hub & Forge
                                       ➔ NO  ➔ Proceed to Next Chamber
```

Post-rebirth, the primary objective is racing back to deep levels where high-yield loot drops. Because your multipliers are drastically higher, gathering elite loot to forge weapons takes a fraction of the time compared to your initial playthrough.

## Step-by-Step Optimization Roadmap

Follow this streamlined roadmap to move from beginner status to endgame dungeon clearing:

1. **Initial Slashing:** Use your hub attack (Left Mouse Button) or the auto-training area to reach baseline Power (approx. 500 Power).
2. **Clear Floors 1–4:** Gather low-tier scrap until your bag is full. Return and craft your first custom Katana or Greatsword.
3. **Expand the Bag:** Sell duplicate drops to buy at least two Bag Capacity upgrades.
4. **Targeted Forging:** Collect rare ore clusters to craft matching Armor and Helmet pieces.
5. **Apply Enchants:** Visit the Enchants station and invest spare Coins to add bonus stat rolls onto your primary weapon.
6. **Hit the Rebirth Gate:** Farm deep floors until your Power matches the current Rebirth requirement, reset, and repeat the cycle with boosted multipliers.

Following this structured loop prevents wasted time and keeps your damage scaling ahead of incoming dungeon threats.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the fastest way to gather high-tier loot to forge weapons?
The most efficient method is upgrading your bag capacity early, using the Four-Hit Rule to stop before dangerous floors, and clearing the deepest safe chamber repeatedly. Pushing into zones where mobs take five or more hits slows down your collection rate and increases the risk of wiping out.

### Should I forge a Katana or a Greatsword first?
For early progression, Greatswords are generally safer because their burst damage clears mobs before they can deal return damage. Once your Power multipliers and passive stats are high enough to eliminate enemies quickly, Katanas offer faster clear speeds through rapid mobility and high swing frequency.

### What happens to my inventory when I Rebirth?
Undergoing a Rebirth resets your current Power score to zero, but you retain critical progression multipliers and access to upgraded systems. You can re-acquire early weapons and advance through the opening dungeon floors much faster thanks to permanent coin and power gain bonuses.

### Can I craft armor using the same materials collected for weapons?
Yes. The ores and materials dropped by dungeon enemies serve both purposes. At the crafting forge, simply toggle over to the Armor tab to roll for Helmets and Chest Armor to boost your survivability.
