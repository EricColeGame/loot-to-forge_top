## Core Forge Modifications: Functions and Unlocks

Before you can forge endgame gear, your basic workstation needs internal component upgrades. A standard forge allows you to melt down iron, brass, lead, and clay, but its crafting speed and recipe library remain strictly limited until you install key tool modifications. 

The three primary forge tools include the **Advanced Bellows**, the **Anvil**, and the **Crucible**. Each tool permanently occupies one of the three dedicated modification slots in the forge interface. You can track your overall progression and game patches directly through the official [7 Days to Die Steam Community Hub](https://store.steampowered.com/app/251570/7_Days_to_Die/), where developers frequently update item balances.

| Forge Tool | Primary Function | Primary Crafting Materials | "Forge Ahead" Magazine Level Needed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Advanced Bellows** | Increases smelting and crafting speed by 50% | Wood, Leather, Iron Pipes, Nails | 20 Magazines |
| **Anvil** | Boosts crafting speed by 25% and unlocks select iron recipes | Iron, Clay | 25 Magazines |
| **Crucible** | Unlocks Forged Steel recipes and high-tier vehicle parts | Forged Iron, Mechanical Parts, Small Engine, Cooking Pot | 75 Magazines |

Installing both the Advanced Bellows and Anvil stacks their crafting speed bonuses, allowing you to mass-produce concrete mix components, forged iron, and bullet casings in a fraction of the standard time. However, the Crucible remains the most coveted prize because forged steel is required for Tier 5 and Tier 6 fortifications, motorcycle chassis, and gyro copter frames.

### High-Priority Containers

Community reports indicate that general home loot rarely yields workstation modifications. Instead, focus your scavenging runs entirely on utility-focused crates:

*   **Working Stiffs Crates:** Found in hardware stores, construction sites, and factory roofs. These hold the highest base chance to drop complete tool mods.
*   **Damaged Workstations:** Abandoned forges found inside ruined workshops can occasionally be searched for leftover tools before you break them down with a wrench.
*   **Tool Boxes & Truck Beds:** Small red toolboxes and pickup utility beds feature favorable roll chances for mechanical parts, pipes, and occasional anvils.
*   **Loot Bags from Tough Hostiles:** While rare, player experience shows that tough enemy types—such as heavy tourist zombies or infected lumberjacks—can drop utility bags carrying manufactured components.

## "Forge Ahead" Magazine Progression Route

If random drops fail you, crafting your own upgrades is guaranteed through the workstation skill system. In recent versions of *7 Days to Die*, individual skill points no longer directly purchase workstation recipes. Instead, you must read copies of the magazine titled **Forge Ahead**.

Accumulating magazines converts your daily **loot to forge crafting** goals into guaranteed milestones. To rush these magazines, you must understand where educational loot spawns most frequently.

| Milestone Level | Unlocked Recipe / Advantage | Optimal Farming Locations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 5** | Basic Forge | Broken Workstations, Cement Mixers |
| **Level 10** | Iron Tools & Cooking Grill | Kitchen Cabinets, Residential Bookshelves |
| **Level 20** | Advanced Bellows | Post Offices, Crack-a-Book POIs |
| **Level 25** | Anvil | Crack-a-Book Displays, School Libraries |
| **Level 50** | Workbench & Cement Mixer | Industrial Garages, Mechanics POIs |
| **Level 75** | Crucible | Crack-a-Book HQ, High-Tier Research Labs |

### Strategies to Target Magazines

1.  **Prioritize Crack-a-Book POIs:** Even small commercial strip-mall bookshops contain dozens of magazine displays. Clear these on every loot reset.
2.  **Loot Mailboxes and Newsstands:** Residential streets are lined with blue mailboxes and newspaper stands. Looting them takes mere seconds and frequently yields "Forge Ahead" issues.
3.  **Invest in Perks:** Putting early points into *Lockpicking* and *Advanced Engineering* (under the Intelligence tree) passively skews world loot rolls toward workstation and electrical crafting magazines.

## Step-by-Step Blueprint for Smelting Forged Steel

Once you acquire a Crucible—whether from lucky exploration or trader trading—you must configure your smelting pipeline correctly. Forged steel requires high temperatures, substantial input materials, and a balanced fuel supply.

### Material Conversion Ratios

To craft steel efficiently, balance your raw resource extraction. Scrap iron cannot be placed directly into standard recipes; it must first melt into the forge repository.

| Final Output | Input Material 1 | Input Material 2 | Fuel Burn Time Needed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Forged Iron (x1)** | 12 Iron Ore / Scrap | 6 Clay Soil | ~10 Seconds (Wood) |
| **Forged Steel (x1)** | 20 Iron Ore / Scrap | 10 Clay Soil | ~25 Seconds (Coal/Wood) |
| **Steel Arrowhead (x5)**| 1 Forged Steel | 2 Clay Soil | ~8 Seconds |
| **Spring (x1)** | 10 Iron Ore | 5 Clay Soil | ~5 Seconds |

### Step-by-Step Operation Process

1.  **Install the Crucible:** Open your forge UI and drag the Crucible into the right-hand tool slot. Verify the icon illuminates.
2.  **Stock Raw Iron and Clay:** Place raw iron (from mining nodes or broken-down scrap) and clay soil into the "Smelting" input slots on the upper-right panel.
3.  **Add Fuel:** Place coal, wood, or wooden furniture in the fuel bay. Turn the forge on to melt materials into the internal reservoir.
4.  **Queue Crafting:** Select "Forged Steel" from the recipe directory on the left. Input your desired batch amount and click "Craft."
5.  **Manage Heat (Screamer Mitigation):** Running multiple forges simultaneously raises regional "chunk heat." Keep a defensive perimeter or install perimeter blade traps to deal with attracted scout screamers before they summon larger hordes.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I find a Crucible directly in world containers?
Yes, but the drop chance is exceptionally low. Crucibles are restricted to high-tier industrial loot tables, such as Tier IV or Tier V Working Stiffs crates, construction site safes, and rarely in damaged forges. Most players obtain their first Crucible either by purchasing it from Trader Bob or Hugh, or by finding 75 "Forge Ahead" magazines.

### What is the fastest method to turn scavenged loot to forge crafting materials?
The fastest method is wrenching down city infrastructure. Dismantle ruined cars, office air vents, kitchen sinks, and street lamps. This produces clean forged iron, pipes, mechanical components, and brass, bypassing the early-game furnace processing times required when digging raw dirt and iron veins.

### Do forge tool mods stack if I install multiples?
No, forges have three distinct, dedicated tool slots: one for an Anvil, one for Bellows, and one for a Crucible. You cannot stack two Anvils or two Crucibles inside the same forge. However, you can build multiple separate forges, equipping each one with duplicate modifications to double your overall hourly production output.

### What perks increase my chances of finding forge parts and books?
Putting points into **Advanced Engineering** (found under the Intelligence attribute tree) directly improves the roll rate of workstation magazines like "Forge Ahead" when opening mailboxes, book cases, and crates. Additionally, raising **Lockpicking** boosts your odds of finding engineering-related publications in toolboxes and mechanic shelving.
