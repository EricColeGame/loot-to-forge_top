## What Is the Gem Forge and How Does It Work?

The Gem Forge is a dedicated crafting station explicitly designed to elevate your socketed items to their maximum potential. Rather than relying purely on RNG drops in adventure worlds, the forge allows players to directly augment stat percentages and swap unwanted affixes for preferred damage multipliers.

When you open the Gem Forge interface, you can select any equipped gem or browse items directly from your adventure inventory. The station displays the exact numerical values of your gem, accompanied by an intuitive visual color-coding system:

- **Green Text (75% to 100%):** High-tier roll. These require very few augmentations to hit the 100% maximum cap.
- **Yellow Text (26% to 74%):** Average roll. These require a moderate investment of crafting foci.
- **Red Text (25% and below):** Low roll. These demand a substantial number of resources to reach max potential.

The interface also tracks stat boost pips (small visual markers granted at level milestones 5, 10, and 15). A gem that started with three base stats can showcase up to three boost markers, signaling top-tier endgame foundation material.

To craft the workstation itself, players must use the Builder’s Crafting Bench under the "Gem & Gear Improvement" tab.

| Required Material | Quantity Needed | Primary Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Infinium** | 30 | Mining in Candoria, Neon City, or Prime Worlds |
| **Water Gem Dust** | 100 | Loot collecting Water Gems from Drowned Worlds |
| **Builder's Crafting Bench** | Station | Found in Hub or crafted at Novice Crafting Station |

## Rerolling Stats with Contained Chaos Sparks

Not every high-rarity gem drops with your build's ideal stat combination. If your magic damage class receives a gem packed with physical damage or maximum health, you do not need to scrap it. Instead, you can craft a **Contained Chaos Spark** to reroll the unwanted stat into a viable alternative.

When you apply a Chaos Spark at the forge:
1. The target stat is wiped and randomly replaced by another attribute from the available pool.
2. The game prevents duplicate or conflicting stats (e.g., if a gem already possesses Physical Damage, it cannot roll Magic Damage unless Physical Damage itself is being rerolled).
3. If an older legacy gem holds Health Regeneration, community reports confirm that using a Chaos Spark removes it permanently, ensuring modern offensive or defensive stats take its place.

| Base Stat Selected | Potential Reroll Outcomes | Build Priority Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Physical Damage** | Critical Hit, Critical Damage, Maximum Health | Keep for Physical DPS; reroll for Magic builds |
| **Magic Damage** | Critical Hit, Critical Damage, Maximum Health | Keep for Magic DPS; reroll for Physical builds |
| **Maximum Health %** | Crit Hit, Crit Damage, Flat Health, Damage Type | High priority for Tanks; reroll for Glass Cannons |
| **Critical Hit** | Damage Type, Critical Damage, Health Pools | Keep until capped (100% CH), then reroll surplus |

Because Contained Chaos Sparks also consume Bound Brilliance, player experience suggests reserving your sparks for rare Empowered Gems first, as farming replacement Lesser Gems from standard elemental worlds is significantly easier.

## Step-by-Step Priority: Upgrading Before Augmenting

A frequent mistake made by newer adventurers is spending valuable **loot to forge gems** on under-leveled or low-rarity items. Because augment percentages scale based on the gem's level and tier, forging too early burns valuable materials that could yield far greater returns later.

Follow this progression pipeline to ensure maximum resource efficiency:

```
[Farm Stellar/Crystal Gem] ➔ [Level to Max (Level 25+)] ➔ [Inspect Stat Rolls (Colors)] ➔ [Use Chaos Sparks (Fix Stats)] ➔ [Apply Foci (Augment to 100%)]
```

### 1. Secure Stellar or Crystal Base Gems
Never use foci on Shadow or Radiant tiers. Lower-ranked gear is quickly outclassed in Uber-9 and Uber-10 worlds. Wait until you acquire Stellar or Crystal drops that spawn with three base stats at Level 1.

### 2. Level the Gem to Cap First
Always level your gem to its maximum breakpoint before visiting the forge. Leveling unlocks stat boosts at levels 5, 10, and 15. Knowing which stats received the critical upgrade procs allows you to make informed reroll and augment choices.

### 3. Stat Rectification Over Stat Boosting
Always reroll flawed stats using Chaos Sparks *before* using your foci. Boosting an unwanted health stat only to reroll it later wastes all the foci previously invested into that slot.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most important loot to forge gems efficiently?
The single most critical resource is Bound Brilliance, as it is required to craft both Builder’s Foci (stat augments) and Contained Chaos Sparks (stat rerolls). Because Bound Brilliance is time-gated behind daily challenges and weekly events, managing it carefully dictates how quickly you can maximize your Power Rank.

### Should I use my loot to forge gems on Radiant or Shadow gems?
No. You should completely avoid spending augment loot on any gem below Stellar or Crystal rarity. Lower-tier gems are temporary stepping stones and will eventually be replaced, meaning any foci or sparks applied to them will be lost permanently.

### How do I know when a gem stat has reached 100%?
Inside the Gem Forge user interface, each stat displays an associated percentage bar. A stat marked with green text that hits 100% has attained its absolute maximum value. At that point, the interface will no longer allow you to apply Builder’s Foci to that specific attribute.

### Can using loot to forge gems downgrade my stats if a roll fails?
No. Gem forging features guaranteed progression. Builder’s Foci always increase your stat percentages by fixed amounts without any chance of failure or regression. While Contained Chaos Sparks reroll your attribute to a random alternative, they never reduce the underlying tier or level of your gem.
