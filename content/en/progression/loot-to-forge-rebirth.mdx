## How the Rebirth System Works in +1 Loot to Forge

At its core, performing a rebirth functions like a traditional prestige mechanic found across modern incremental games. You trade your current accumulated Power stat in exchange for permanent account-wide bonuses. While watching your hard-earned Power drop down to zero can feel intimidating at first, the underlying math heavily favors players who cycle through rebirth tiers without hesitation.

When you trigger a reset, two foundational multipliers are permanently applied to your profile:
*   **Power Gain Multiplier:** Increases the raw Power you earn per hit or training tick.
*   **Coin Earnings Multiplier:** Boosts gold yields from all drops, chest loot, and quest rewards.

In addition to pure numerical boosts, completing rebirth milestones unlocks access to higher-tier training dummies. These advanced targets dramatically accelerate your stat acquisition compared to starter equipment.

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Training Dummies** | New Tiers Unlocked | Provides higher base yields per strike |

According to player experience reported across community hubs, top-tier weapons and forged artifacts do not disappear when resetting. This balance decision ensures that your retained gear cuts down subsequent leveling loops from hours to mere seconds.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the primary benefit of a loot to forge rebirth?
Triggering a rebirth resets your raw Power stat to zero in exchange for permanent multipliers to your Power gain rate and Coin earnings. It also unlocks higher-level training dummies that allow you to regain lost stats significantly faster than before.

### Do I lose my forged items when I rebirth?
No. You keep your unlocked tools, crafted weapons, and forged equipment. Your inventory assets remain untouched, allowing your high-tier gear to quickly carry your Power level back to maximum.

### Can I bank multiple rebirths at once?
Currently, the system does not support bulk rebirths. If you continue training after the rebirth meter reaches 100%, any surplus Power earned does not count toward a second reset. It is best to reset as soon as you meet the requirement.

### How do I know when I am ready to rebirth?
Check the rebirth tracker on the left side of your screen. When the progress percentage reaches 100%, you have met the minimum Power and level requirements to claim your next set of permanent multipliers.
