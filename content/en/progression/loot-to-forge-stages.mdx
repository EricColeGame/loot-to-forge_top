## Core Progression Loop: How Stages Work

The overall gameplay loop in *+1 Loot to Forge* revolves around entering a segmented, linear dungeon hallway, defeating enemies to claim their raw materials, and bringing those ores back to the central hub forge. Each segment acts as a discrete level or stage, presenting a sharp difficulty jump compared to the one before it.

Inside the dungeon, your character attacks automatically, freeing you to focus on mobility, ability cooldowns (the **Q** key), and threat assessment. The primary bottleneck isn't just enemy health—it is your backpack capacity. Mobs drop valuable crafting components, but once your inventory fills up, you cannot accumulate additional items until you return to base to forge or liquidate excess inventory for Coins.

```
Hub Prep (Train / Forge / Enchant) ➔ Enter Dungeon ➔ Clear Stage Levels ➔ Maximize Bag Capacity ➔ Tactical Retreat ➔ Upgrade & Rebirth
```

Progressing smoothly requires balancing your weapon tier with armor crafting and passive base power. If you push into deeper levels without meeting the defensive and offensive thresholds, you risk fainting and losing efficiency.

### When to Push vs. When to Retreat

Combat is designed around steady pacing. A reliable rule of thumb popularized by seasoned players is the **Four-Hit Benchmark**:

* **Safe Farming:** Enemies die in 1 to 2 basic swings. You can safely sustain this stage to farm high quantities of baseline ores.
* **Optimal Progression:** Enemies die in 3 to 4 hits. Your incoming damage is manageable, and drop value per minute remains high.
* **Danger Zone:** If a normal mob requires 5 or more hits to defeat, immediately stop moving forward. The power gap into the next sub-stage will likely overwhelm you. Cash in your bag and return to the hub.

### Armor and Helmets: Mitigating RNG

In addition to forging swords, visiting the Armor tab at the forge is mandatory for surviving later **loot to forge stages**. Armor directly increases your maximum Power pool and defense. Keep in mind that crafting armor relies on random generation—you cannot always pick whether you receive a Helmet or a Chestpiece. 

* **Helmets:** Grant offensive Power multipliers and passive skill haste.
* **Chest Armor:** Dramatically increases flat health pools and physical damage reduction.

If you roll duplicate pieces, break them down or sell them for Coins to reinvest into your enchantments and bag storage.

## Training, Enchantments, and Rebirth Systems

When raw gear upgrades alone fail to pierce the toughest **loot to forge stages**, you must utilize secondary progression systems: base training, station enchantments, and the rebirth mechanic.

### Manual vs. Auto-Training

Your base Power acts as the foundational multiplier applied to all your equipped gear. You have two primary ways to train:
1. **Manual Slashing (Hub):** Left-clicking in the hub grants quick bursts of base power.
2. **Auto-Training Zone:** Stepping into the designated passive circle allows your character to continuously gain power without manual input. While auto-training yields slightly lower power-per-second than hyper-active clicking, it provides an effortless way to prepare for difficult dungeon pushes while AFK.

### Enchantment Stat Priorities

Before entering mid-tier or endgame floors, spend excess Coins at the Enchants Station. Enchantments apply rolling percentage modifiers to your equipped sword:

* **Sharpness (Tier I–V):** Flat damage percentage increase; universally top-tier.
* **Looter’s Fortune (Tier I–III):** Boosts the drop rate of rare gems (Garnet, Sapphire, Ruby).
* **Vampirism (Tier I–IV):** Restores a fraction of health on enemy elimination, drastically lowering the risk of dying.

### Maximizing Rebirth Efficiency

Rebirthing is the definitive engine driving long-term progression across modern incremental games. Once you attain a specified threshold of total Power, you can execute a Rebirth.

```
Reach Required Power ➔ Rebirth Reset ➔ Retain Permanent Multipliers ➔ Rapidly Clear Early Stages
```

Executing a rebirth temporarily resets your active Power back to baseline, but your upgraded multipliers allow you to breeze through early **loot to forge stages** in a fraction of the time, unlocking deeper dungeon segments that were previously impossible to conquer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if I die inside the dungeon?
If your health reaches zero inside the dungeon, your run ends immediately. Depending on your current checkpoint and game updates, you risk forfeiting the unbanked materials stored inside your bag during that run. Always monitor enemy damage and retreat before entering stages where enemies take more than four hits to defeat.

### Which weapon type is better for clearing early loot to forge stages: Katana or Greatsword?
For early **loot to forge stages** (Floors 1 through 12), the Greatsword is generally preferred due to its broad sweeping area-of-effect skill, which dispatches tightly clustered mob groups quickly. However, once you reach higher-tier floors with resilient elite enemies, Katanas become exceptionally powerful due to their single-target burst damage and short skill cooldowns.

### How do I reliably acquire armor pieces instead of helmets at the forge?
Armor and helmet crafting share the same material queue under the blacksmith's Armor tab. The specific drop is governed by RNG (random number generation). The most effective community-tested strategy is to hoard enough materials for 4 to 6 crafts simultaneously, ensuring you hit at least one full matched set of gear.

### When should I trigger my first Rebirth?
You should execute your first rebirth as soon as your progression noticeably slows down in Stage 5 (around Floor 35). The permanent 100% boost to power generation and 50% coin bonus will allow you to reclaim your previous floor progress within minutes, giving you the stat pool needed to break into deep dungeon floors.
