## Core Mechanics: How Forging and Loot Multipliers Work

Forging in *The Forge* is not a simple random number generator; it relies on a transparent mathematical formula that combines your weapon base stats, raw material purity, and weapon mastery. To maximize damage output, you cannot simply rely on late-game blueprints alone—you must pair them with optimal ores.

According to developer documentation and testing, the core damage and value scaling formula operates as follows:


> **Formula:** Total Damage = Index Base Damage x Ore Multiplier x Mastery Item %


Every single percentage point of Mastery contributes a compounding boost to your gear. Specifically, the mastery item percentage provides an extra 1.01x multiplier for every 1% of mastery you possess. This means bringing the correct loot to forge rare weapons into your anvil sessions yields exponentially better stats when your mastery levels are pushed to their limits.

## Optimized Farming Routes for Crafting Materials

To efficiently acquire the loot to forge rare weapons, you must establish an optimized farming route. Wandering aimlessly between nodes slows down your progression.

Follow this step-by-step route to keep your inventories stocked with high-purity ores and currency:

1. **Equip High-Tier Pickaxes:** Never mine higher-grade ores with a starter pickaxe. Your mining speed directly determines your yield per minute.
2. **Clear Stonewake's Cross Outskirts:** Begin by mining surface veins to build base funds and low-tier ores for early mastery grinding.
3. **Delve Into The Cave:** Move into subterranean caverns where rare mineral veins spawn. Keep an eye out for enemy patrols that guard cluster deposits.
4. **Target Elite Mobs for Blueprint Drops:** Eliminate named elites along your mining path to combine ore farming with blueprint chances.
5. **Batch Craft for Mastery:** Avoid crafting weapons one by one. Accumulate enough loot to forge rare weapons in batches of 20 to 50 items. This rapidly elevates your mastery percentage, ensuring your best weapon attempts benefit from the compounding 1.01x mastery multiplier.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the single best loot to forge rare weapons in the game?
The most valuable materials include rare subterranean ores from The Cave combined with elite blueprint drops. High-purity ores provide substantial base multipliers that scale dramatically when paired with late-game blueprints like Excalibur or the Great Sword.

### What is the rarest weapon you can craft?
The Excalibur is the rarest craftable weapon in the game, featuring a base crafting chance of 1 in 1,000. Players must invest significant amounts of high-tier loot to forge rare weapons of this caliber, as multiple crafting attempts are typically necessary.

### Does weapon mastery carry over between different weapons?
Mastery increases your overall proficiency multiplier (1.01x per 1% mastery), which applies directly to the damage calculation formula for all weapons you forge. Raising your mastery level with inexpensive weapons first is an effective strategy before spending rare materials on top-tier gear.

### Can you craft all weapons in every zone?
No. Certain weapon types are location-locked. For instance, Boxing Gloves can only be crafted in Stonewake's Cross and cannot be forged within the Forgotten Kingdom. Always verify zone requirements before farming regional materials.
