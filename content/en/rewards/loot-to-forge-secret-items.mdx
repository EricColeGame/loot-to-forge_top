## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the fastest way to get rare loot to forge secret items?
The fastest method is to push as deep into the linear dungeon as your weapon allows without exceeding the four-hit-per-enemy threshold. Higher floors have exponentially higher drop rates for rare and secret-tier ores. Pair deep-floor runs with expanded backpack upgrades so you do not have to leave early.

### Should I forge a Katana or a Greatsword for secret crafting?
It depends on your playstyle. Katanas excel at rapid-fire dungeon clearing thanks to fast attack speeds and dynamic dash skills on the `Q` key. Greatswords hit significantly harder per swing, making them ideal for bursting down resilient mobs on deep floors.

### Do I lose my weapons and gear when I Rebirth?
A Rebirth resets your raw Power counter back to zero, but your permanent multipliers to coin collection and Power generation increase significantly. Check your current tier requirements in the Rebirth menu before resetting to confirm the net boost.

### How do I get more Coins to pay for bag upgrades and weapon enchants?
Sell surplus, low-tier mob drops and common ores that you no longer need at the hub merchant. As your Rebirth multiplier climbs, standard coin payouts from clearing dungeon floors and selling scrap loot multiply accordingly.
